About PlantCare

PlantCare is an AI-based Plant Disease Detection website that helps users identify plant diseases quickly and accurately. Users can upload a leaf image, and the system analyzes it to detect the disease and provide treatment suggestions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support farmers and gardeners by providing fast, easy, and reliable plant disease detection using Artificial Intelligence.

Features

  • 🌿 Upload Plant Image
  • 🤖 AI Disease Detection
  • 💊 Treatment Suggestions
  • 📊 Easy to Use Interface
